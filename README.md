# SunScore
**Problem**: Families, hostels and departments hear about rooftop solar but don't know how much their roof could
generate, which panel material suits them, or how long it takes to pay back. Installers' quotes are hard to compare.

**Features**: An app that takes roof area, direction and shading, estimates yearly solar generation for Hyderabad, compares
monocrystalline, polycrystalline and thin-film panels, shows a simple payback estimate, explains the science, and builds a campus map of solar potential.
